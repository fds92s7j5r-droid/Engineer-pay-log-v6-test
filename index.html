Pay Rules & RatesRate table Jul 29, 2026 • 8-hour minimum • reference details›

View full pay rules
Pay scale: Automatically determined from Employment Start Date using the BLET rate tables effective July 29, 2026 (60%, 80%, 90%, 100%).
Minimum: 8 hours per paid shift.
Normal OT: actual time over 8 hours.
Full-shift OT: Holiday, Short Swing, or Relief Day.
Night differential: actual time between 8:00 PM and 5:59 AM.
Meal Period: 20 minutes at time-and-one-half.
Freight Meal: 40 minutes at time-and-one-half.
Fuel Claim: 30 minutes at time-and-one-half.
Daily Cert: $10 once per normal worked shift; excluded from Special Shifts.
Trainee: 1 hour straight time per trainee, maximum 2.
Operating Without a Conductor: 8 hours straight time.
Yard Certification: 1 hour straight time per certification.
Commingle / Sheridan-Shop-House Move: each adds 8 hours straight time.
2 Classes of Service: compares the 8-hour straight-time payment with ordinary overtime attributable to the pull and uses the greater payment; the same pull is not paid both ways.
Terminal Not In Crew Book: 2 hours straight time.
Runaround Claim: 2 hours straight time plus the entered difference in earnings, all straight time.
Time on Time: variable additional straight-time payment, maximum 8:00 per shift.
Miscellaneous Penalty: user-entered description and duration paid at the selected straight-time or overtime rate.
Vacation Week: 1/52 of previous year gross earnings, minimum five basic days.
Single Vacation Day: 1/5 of the vacation-week value, minimum one basic day.

Enable Net Pay Estimator?

A planning estimate based on your own paycheck history.

Net Pay Estimator uses the average take-home percentage from your last 10 paychecks to estimate future net pay.

Because taxes, deductions, benefits, contributions, withholding, and other payroll items vary from employee to employee, this is an estimate only and is not a guarantee of your actual paycheck.

To use this feature, enter the gross and net amounts from your 10 most recent paychecks. These amounts can be found in the BSC portal. Keeping your paycheck history current will improve the estimate.

What's New in Engineer Pay Log V9.0

V9.0 Crew Book Image Update
V9.0 Crew Book Image Update
The official September 8, 2026 Passenger Crew Book has now been republished by the railroad with Revision 8 incorporated directly into the Crew Book pages. Engineer Pay Log V9.0 now uses these finalized official assignment images in Quick Job Lookup and Job Comparison.

This replaces the temporary separate Paper Revision display used when V9.0 was first released. The underlying Crew Book Assistance data and V9.0 job tools remain unchanged. No action is required.
  • Job Comparison: compare multiple assignments side-by-side by date, terminal, report/release times, relief days, duration, meal status, estimated earnings, and the official printed Crew Book assignment.
  • Quick Job Lookup: enter one assignment and date for a fast Crew Book summary, review the official printed assignment, or send that job directly into Job Comparison.
  • Official Crew Book images: all official assignment images for the September 8, 2026 Crew Book revision are loaded in Engineer Pay Log and will be available for viewing beginning September 8.
  • Crew Book Assistance: remains aligned with the manually certified September 8 data while the official image viewer now uses the finalized up-to-Revision-8 pages.
  • Finalized official Crew Book images: Revision 8 is now incorporated directly into the railroad's official September 8 assignment pages displayed in the app.
Crew Book verification: the certified September 8 assignment data remains unchanged. The newly published integrated Revision 8 pages were checked against the previously certified Paper Revision changes before replacing the temporary image layer.

As always, Crew Book Assistance is provided for convenience. Please verify your assignment against the official Crew Book and current railroad instructions.
