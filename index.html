Employment & Pay

Current Pay Scale: Not set
1st 365 days: 60%
2nd 365 days: 80%
3rd 365 days: 90%
4th 365 days and beyond: 100%
70% is not currently used.

Each saved shift records the rate that applied on that shift date, so moving to a higher rate later will not rewrite previously saved earnings.

Engineer Trainee Mode is active. New shifts use trainee rules.

Install Engineer Pay Log

iPhone / iPad — Safari: Open Engineer Pay Log in Safari → tap Share → Add to Home Screen → Add.

Android — Chrome: Open Engineer Pay Log in Chrome → open the browser menu → Install app or Add to Home screen → confirm.

Android — Firefox: Open Engineer Pay Log in Firefox → open the menu → Install or Add to Home screen → confirm.

Desktop — Chrome / Edge: Use the Install option in the address bar or browser menu when available.

Installing the app does not back up your shifts. Your history remains stored locally on that browser/device. Use Backup JSON regularly.

Pay rules

Pay scale: Automatically determined from Employment Start Date using the BLET rate tables effective July 29, 2026 (60%, 80%, 90%, 100%).
Minimum: 8 hours per paid shift.
Normal OT: actual time over 8 hours.
Full-shift OT: Holiday, Short Swing, or Relief Day.
Night differential: actual time between 8:00 PM and 5:59 AM.
Meal Period: 20 minutes at time-and-one-half.
Freight Meal: 40 minutes at time-and-one-half.
Fuel Claim: 30 minutes at time-and-one-half.
Daily Cert: $10 once per normal worked shift; excluded from Special Shifts.
Trainee: 1 hour straight time per trainee, maximum 2.
Operating Without a Conductor: 8 hours straight time.
Yard Certification: 1 hour straight time per certification.
Commingle / 2 Classes of Service / Sheridan-Shop-House Move: each adds 8 hours straight time.
Terminal Not In Crew Book: 2 hours straight time.
Runaround Claim: 2 hours straight time plus the entered difference in earnings, all straight time.
Time on Time: variable additional straight-time payment, maximum 8:00 per shift.

What's New in Engineer Pay Log 6.0

Crew tracking, new claims, Special Shifts, cleaner pay details, and PDF reports.
Change on the change!
  • Overtime Upon Completion: New claim for work performed immediately after the original job is completed. Enter the original job completion time and Engineer Pay Log calculates the overtime claim through your actual end time.
  • Engineer Trainee Mode: A dedicated trainee workflow with restricted claims, trainee overtime rules, Class entries, Holiday - Not Worked, and instructor tracking.
  • Total Time on Duty: Live read-only duration calculated from start and end time.
  • Time on Time: Capped at a maximum of 8:00 per shift.
  • Backup reminder: Startup reminder after more than 10 days with a one-tap Back Up Now button.
  • New in 5.0.2 — PDF Layout Cleanup: Cleaner spacing, flexible shift cards, dedicated claim/crew/note rows, improved wrapping, and better page flow.
  • New in 5.0.1 — PDF Report Redesign: Shift History exports now use a polished report layout with monthly summaries, clearer shift cards, highlighted Time Slips, and the presenting engineer.
  • Crew tracking: Conductor and Brakeman names can now be saved with each shift.
  • New claims: Freight Meal (40 min at 1.5×) and Fuel Claim (30 min at 1.5×).
  • Daily Cert: $10 automatically added to every normal worked shift.
  • Special Shifts: Sick Day, Paid Guarantee, Two Year Book, Miscellaneous Instruction, Personal Day, and Company Court.
  • Cleaner Calculated Pay: $0.00 lines stay hidden; only earnings that apply are shown.
  • Holiday Pay: updated wording replaces “Holiday Bonus.”
  • PDF Export: create a clean shift-history report in addition to CSV.
