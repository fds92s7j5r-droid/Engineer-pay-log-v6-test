ENGINEER PAY LOG — VERSION 5.0.0

NEW IN 5.0
- Optional Conductor Name and Brakeman Name fields under Misc. Notes.
- Freight Meal: 40 minutes at time-and-one-half.
- Fuel Claim: 30 minutes at time-and-one-half.
- Daily Cert: automatic $10 once per normal worked shift.
- Daily Cert excluded from Sick Day, Paid Guarantee, Two Year Book, Miscellaneous Instruction, Personal Day, and Company Court.
- Sick Day and Paid Guarantee: flat 8 hours straight time.
- Two Year Book, Miscellaneous Instruction, Personal Day, and Company Court:
  * Extra List / Subject to Bid: 1/5 previous pay period qualifying earnings, excluding Daily Cert.
  * Own a Yard / Freight Job: same 1/5 rule.
  * Own a Passenger Job: enter the regular passenger job normally; Daily Cert excluded.
- Duplicate Shift preserves Special Shift type/category and crew names, while variable penalties/Time Slip data reset.
- Calculated Pay hides $0.00 rows and shows only applicable earnings plus Total.
- “Holiday Bonus” renamed “Holiday Pay.”
- What's New uses the existing Engineer Pay Log mascot with “Change on the change!”
- History can export a clean PDF report as well as CSV. PDF respects the active History search/filter.

PRESERVED
- Hire-date 60% / 80% / 90% / 100% pay progression.
- Time Slip Center and yellow Time Slip highlighting.
- Saved Jobs / Favorites.
- Expected Paycheck.
- Backup reminders and Restore Preview.
- All prior penalties and pay rules.
- Local-only storage and PWA support.

Unofficial personal tracking tool. Not an official LIRR, MTA, or union product.
