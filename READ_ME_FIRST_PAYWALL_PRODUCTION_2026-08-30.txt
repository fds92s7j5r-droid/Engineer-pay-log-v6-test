ENGINEER PAY LOG — PAYWALL PRODUCTION — 2026-08-30

Production billing UI based on the fully tested Paywall Sandbox Preview 2.

Behavior:
- 30-day free trial; no card required to begin.
- $5/month subscription.
- Users may subscribe during the trial without losing remaining free time.
- Cancellation preserves access through the current paid/trial period.
- Failed renewal payments receive a 7-day full-access grace period while payment can be corrected.
- After trial/subscription/grace access ends, app becomes read-only; existing data/history/exports remain available.
- Re-subscribing restores full access.
- Creator complimentary access is server-owned.

IMPORTANT: Billing enforcement is controlled server-side in Supabase billing_config.
